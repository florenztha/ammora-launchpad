Create a launch

Plans are generated by the SDK's portable lifecycle planner (planExistingConfigAtomicFirstBuy) against a live-verified deployment. The planner never signs: your wallet reviews and sends each step. Testnet only.

SDK 0.3.9 limitation, stated plainly: the atomic first buy is settled by the protocol in its quote asset (WETH) and needs a WETH approval; your native ETH covers gas and the pool creation fee only. Ammora's SDK offers no native-ETH first buy, and this site does not relabel WETH as ETH. Trading on a launched curve (quote panel) is native-ETH based through the native router.

Curve quote

Native-ETH quotes through the SDK's AmmoraSwapClient and the deployment's native router: for curves Ammora has registered for native routing, you pay and receive native GIWA Sepolia ETH, never WETH. Unregistered curves fail closed and the panel says why. The panel also shows the curve's own reference quote, denominated in the protocol quote asset the curve custodies internally.
